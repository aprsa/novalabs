# Celestial Sphere Briefing

This lab introduces how Earth's rotation and revolution shape the night sky. You will explore:

- Apparent daily motion of stars due to Earth's rotation
- Seasonal changes in daylight and constellations
- The Sun's annual path along the ecliptic
- The ~4-minute offset between sidereal and solar days

Use the embedded Stellarium view to adjust latitude, time, and view direction. Record observations carefully—you will need them for the quizzes and exercises.
